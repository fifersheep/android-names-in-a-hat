Privacy Policy

Names in a Hat for Android (uk.lobsterdoodle.namepicker)
Last updated: 19 August 2026

The short version: Names in a Hat does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. Everything you type into the app — draw titles and names — is stored only on your own device. There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics, and no third-party tracking of any kind, for children or adults alike.

1. Who this policy is from

Names in a Hat is developed and published by Northtrack Studio (Scott Laing), the developer account responsible for the app on Google Play. The app was previously published under the name Lobster Doodle; the developer name has changed, but the app, its handling of your information, and the person behind it have not. This policy explains how the Android app handles information. It applies to the app itself and to every version of it distributed through Google Play.

The app's package name, uk.lobsterdoodle.namepicker, still carries the former name. A package name cannot be changed once an app is listed on Google Play, so it stays as it is; it is an internal identifier and says nothing about who operates the app or how your information is handled.

2. Information the app collects

None. The app does not collect any personal or sensitive user data. Specifically, it does not collect or transmit:

  • names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other contact details;
  • accounts, sign-in credentials, or user profiles (the app has no sign-in at all);
  • location data of any kind;
  • device identifiers, advertising IDs, or persistent identifiers;
  • photos, files, contacts, microphone or camera data;
  • usage analytics, event data, crash reports, or diagnostic data;
  • payment or financial information.

The app requests no Android runtime permissions, and it contains no code that sends information off your device.

3. Information the app stores on your device

To do its job, the app saves what you enter in local storage on your device only. This information never leaves the device by way of the app.

What is storedWhyWhere
Draw titles and the names you add to each draw So your lists are still there the next time you open the app A private app database on your device
Small app preferences (for example the last draw you opened and your chosen column layout) So the app reopens the way you left it Private app preferences on your device

You choose what to type in. The app does not require real names — nicknames, initials, or made-up labels work just as well.

4. Device backup

Android offers a system-level backup feature. If you have turned backup on for your device, Android may include this app's local data in the backup that is stored in your own Google account. That copy is made by Android, not by the app, and it is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. The developer has no access to it. You can turn device backup off at any time in your Android system settings.

5. No advertising, analytics, or third-party SDKs

The app displays no adverts and contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, and no social or messaging SDK. No data is shared with, or sold to, any third party, because no data is collected in the first place. There are no in-app purchases and no in-app browser that loads third-party content.

The app may offer a link to open this privacy policy in your device's browser. Following that link is a normal web request handled by your browser, and it is your choice whether to make it.

6. Children's privacy

Names in a Hat is a simple, general-audience utility that is also suitable for children and families — it is often used in classrooms and at home to pick names at random. We take that audience seriously:

  • the app collects no personal information from anyone, of any age;
  • nothing a child (or an adult) types is transmitted anywhere — it stays on the device;
  • there is no advertising, no behavioural profiling, and no tracking;
  • there is no chat, no user-generated content sharing, and no social features;
  • there are no links to external content other than this privacy policy.

Because no personal information is ever collected, none is knowingly collected from children. The app is designed to meet the requirements of the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the UK and EU GDPR provisions on children's data, Google Play's Families Policy Requirements, and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code.

7. How to review or delete your information

Because everything lives on your device, you are always in control of it:

  • delete an individual name or an entire draw from within the app;
  • clear all app data at once via Settings → Apps → Names in a Hat → Storage → Clear storage;
  • uninstall the app, which removes its local data from the device.

There is no server-side copy to request, export, or erase, and no account to close. If device backup is switched on, you can also remove backed-up app data through your Google account's backup settings.

8. Security

App data is held in the app's private storage area, which Android isolates from other apps. Since nothing is transmitted, there is no data in transit to intercept and no server-side store to breach.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this policy, the policy will be updated here before the change ships, and the "Last updated" date at the top will change. Material changes will also be noted in the app's Google Play release notes. This page will remain available for as long as the app is listed on Google Play.
